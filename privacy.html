Privacy Policy

DAN IPTV · Last updated October 05, 2026

DAN IPTV does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What stays on your device

  • Your provider login (server, username, password or playlist link) is stored only on your device, in the iOS Keychain.
  • Favorites, resume positions, settings and the downloaded TV guide are stored only on your device.

Network connections

The app connects only to the addresses you enter (your provider's servers and guide links) to load channels, guide data, artwork and video. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any analytics, advertising or tracking service.

No tracking

DAN IPTV contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. Questions: contact support.
