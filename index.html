DAN IPTV

A player for your own IPTV subscription on iPhone and iPad.

DAN IPTV does not provide any channels, movies or other content. You sign in with the Xtream Codes login or M3U playlist link supplied by your own TV provider.

Getting started

  1. Open the app and choose Xtream Codes or M3U playlist.
  2. Enter the server address, username and password (or the playlist link) from your provider.
  3. Your channels, movies, series and TV guide load automatically.

Try it without a subscription

Features

  • Live TV with a full programme guide (XMLTV)
  • Movies and series with resume
  • Multiview: up to four channels at once
  • Favorites, search and six colour themes
  • AirPlay to Apple TV

Common questions

A channel says "Can't start this stream". The card explains what your provider answered. Most often the account is already in use on another device. Tap Send log to share the details.

The guide is empty. Add your provider's XMLTV link under Settings → Custom EPG URL.
